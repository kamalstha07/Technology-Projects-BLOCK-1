# Part 2 – Computer Disassembly
## Before Opening the Computer
Describe the computer before opening it.
The computer is a standard desktop tower in a black case. On the front, it has a power button, audio jacks, and a few USB ports for quick access. Moving to the back, you can see all the primary connection ports, including the main power input, display ports for the monitor, and extra USB inputs for a keyboard and mouse. You can also see ventilation slots along the sides and rear to help keep air moving through the system.

## Internal Layout
Open the computer case and observe the internal components.
Describe what you see.
After removing the side panel, the internal parts are completely visible. The main circuit board mounted against the back wall is the motherboard, which connects everything together. Near the center, you can see the CPU covered by a cooling fan and heatsink, with memory sticks (RAM) fitted into nearby slots. Down in the corner, the power supply unit feeds power to the entire system through a bundle of cables, while the storage drives sit neatly in their drive bays.
